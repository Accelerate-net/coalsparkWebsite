Our story

Coalspark is a standout destination in town for exceptional Arabic cuisine and BBQ, quickly earning its place as a popular choice for Middle Eastern flavors alongside Indian and Chinese favorites. Every dish is crafted with passion and precision, bringing authentic Arabian and Indian tastes that truly delight the palate.

We offer a diverse menu featuring North Indian, Chinese, Seafood, and Arabian specialties - each prepared using fresh ingredients to deliver rich, satisfying flavors. The restaurant’s vibrant and inviting ambiance enhances the dining experience, while our thoughtfully curated, pocket friendly menu ensures great taste in every bite. Our barbeque selections are a highlight, but it’s our signature Arabic dishes that truly define Coalspark and make it a must-visit for food lovers.

Locations

Bangalore | Chennai | Hyderabad

Driven by a strong commitment to quality and consistency, Coalspark has proudly expanded its presence across Bangalore, Chennai, and Hyderabad. At every location, we ensure that both food and service meet the highest standards, creating a dining experience that is always enjoyable and reliable.

We are deeply focused on preserving the authentic taste and quality of our cuisine, while also offering a refreshing and inviting ambience - perfect for everything from a quick, impromptu meal to a relaxed dining experience. For your special moments, Coalspark’s banquet hall provides the ideal venue, with dedicated arrangements to host and cater to all kinds of celebrations and events.

We are open from 11:30 AM to 12 Midnight
